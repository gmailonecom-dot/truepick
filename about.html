About TruePick

An affiliate picks site — we choose the products, you buy them where you trust.

Last updated: 16 October 2026

What TruePick is

TruePick is a curated affiliate storefront. We don't hold stock, and we don't take your payment. Instead we hand-pick products we think are genuinely worth buying, explain who each one is right for, compare the live price across trusted stores, and send you straight to that store to complete the purchase.

Why we built it

Affiliate shopping usually means one long list of links with no guidance. TruePick adds the missing part — the honest "is this actually for me?" answer. Every product page says who it suits, how to check whether it fits you, what to do if it doesn't, and what to buy instead. You decide; the store handles the sale.

How we choose products

  • We look at real ratings and review volume, not just the seller's marketing.
  • We check the price on more than one store, so you can pick the better deal.
  • We write the "good for these people" section ourselves, in plain language.
  • If a product isn't right for a group of people, we say so on the page.

How we make money

When you click a "Buy on Amazon" or "Buy on Flipkart" button and go on to buy, the store may pay us a small commission. It costs you nothing extra — the price you pay is the same. That commission is what keeps TruePick running, and it never changes which products we recommend or how we describe them.

Your data

Your wishlist and your product ratings are stored in your own browser, on your device. We don't require an account to browse. See our Terms & conditions for the full detail.
